Business & Financial Communities & Corporations.

This page provides a framework within which activists may work to promote Common-Law & Constitutional-Republican principles within Business, Financial, & Corporate communities. Responsible commerce & sound financial stewardship are foundational to any self-governing republic. Activists are encouraged to engage within their respective business communities to promote accountability, transparency, & compliance with natural law & common-law principles.

Major Business & Financial Community Categories:

The following represent the principal categories of business & financial communities within which organizing efforts are encouraged. All such communities are invited to participate in good-faith dialogue & consensus-building toward responsible, Common-Law-compliant commercial practices.

  • Small Businesses & Local Enterprises.

    The backbone of any self-governing republic. Locally owned & operated businesses are especially important to the economic independence & resilience of each of the Twelve Super-State communities. Activists are encouraged to support & patronize locally owned enterprises wherever possible.

  • Corporations & Multi-National Enterprises.

    Large corporate entities wield significant influence over economic & political life. Activists within these organizations are encouraged to promote accountability to communities, transparency in governance, & compliance with natural-law & common-law principles in all commercial dealings.

  • Banking & Financial Institutions.

    Sound money, honest banking, & transparent financial practices are essential to a self-governing republic. Activists are encouraged to promote reform within banking & financial institutions to ensure they serve the genuine needs of local communities rather than centralized special interests.

  • Agricultural & Land-Based Enterprises.

    Food production & land stewardship are among the most fundamental economic activities of any community. Activists in agricultural communities are encouraged to promote local food sovereignty, sustainable land practices, & fair market access for family farmers & ranchers.

  • Trade & Professional Guilds & Associations.

    Voluntary associations of skilled tradespeople & professionals provide important community functions in setting standards, training, & mutual aid. Such organizations are encouraged to align their practices with common-law principles of voluntary association & local accountability.

  • Cooperative & Mutual-Aid Organizations.

    Worker cooperatives, credit unions, mutual insurance societies, & similar community-owned enterprises embody many of the principles of responsible self-governance. Such organizations are especially welcomed in the Common-Law Republic organizing framework.

  • Investment & Capital Markets Communities.

    Those who steward capital & investment have significant responsibilities to the communities in which they operate. Activists in investment & capital markets are encouraged to promote transparency, long-term community benefit, & compliance with common-law principles in all financial dealings.

  • Technology & Innovation Enterprises.

    Technological innovation has transformed commerce & communication. Activists in technology communities are encouraged to promote innovation that empowers individuals & local communities rather than concentrating power in distant centralized entities.

Principles for Common-Law-Compliant Commerce:

All business & financial communities operating within the Common-Law Republic framework are encouraged to observe the following principles:

  • Honest weights & measures; full transparency in all dealings & contracts.
  • Accountability to local communities & constituencies, not only to distant shareholders.
  • Respect for the property rights & economic freedoms of individuals & families.
  • Good-faith participation in community organizing & consensus-building efforts.
  • Avoidance of monopolistic practices & protection of free & fair local markets.
  • Support for sound money principles & honest banking practices.
